PCOps Support

Support is provided directly by the developer during the current release-preparation phase.

Contact

support@pcopsassistant.com

What to include

  • PCOps Assistant version.
  • Your Windows version.
  • The exact error or symptom.
  • A screenshot if relevant.
  • Whether the issue occurs during install, launch, diagnostic, update review or uninstall.
Do not send passwords, Windows product keys, software license keys, payment-card information or unrelated personal files.

Release downloads

The public PCOps Free download and its integrity information will be published here only after the current release passes the final publication gate.
