One place to see what matters

PCOps is designed for people who want useful answers, not another wall of raw system data.

Unified diagnostics

Review operating system, hardware, installed RAM, storage, drivers, startup programs, Windows Update readiness, installed software and system-health signals from one interface.

History and changes

Save diagnostic history locally and compare scans to understand what changed over time.

Safety-first actions

Diagnostic scans are read-only. Actions that can change the system are separated from diagnosis and require explicit authorization and safety checks.

Plain-language findings

PCOps turns technical observations into prioritized findings with context for the user.

Local-first privacy

The core diagnostic workflow runs locally. PCOps does not require an account, telemetry or analytics for normal diagnostic use.

Independent development

PCOps is developed by Alexis Bernal with a pioneering, safety-driven approach to PC diagnostics for the AI era.

Local by default

Saved diagnostic history stays on your computer in a local database. PCOps does not read the contents of personal documents, browsing history, passwords or Windows product keys during normal diagnostics.

Optional update actions may invoke Windows or WinGet services, which can use the network when you explicitly request an update-related action.
