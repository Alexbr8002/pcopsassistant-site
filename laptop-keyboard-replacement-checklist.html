Keyboard guide

Laptop keyboard replacement checklist

Fits this brand is not enough for an internal keyboard.

Exact model first

Use the exact laptop model and, when available, the manufacturer part identifier. Similar chassis generations can use different ribbon connectors or mounting systems.

Layout, backlight and frame

Check language, Enter-key shape, numeric keypad, backlight support, color and whether the replacement is keyboard-only or an entire top-case assembly.

Repairability matters

Review a service manual before ordering. Some keyboards are simple screw-and-ribbon replacements while others are integrated into the palm rest.
